# Six name groups (headline Figure 3)

Black vs White rows are the confirmatory H1 and H4. Every other row, and the omnibus test, is exploratory and was added after the main results were seen. Price: gap vs White names for the same clear request (S0), name level, 95% CI, in percent and in dollars per purchase at the White names' average price paid (Table 1). Identity-linked pick: vague request, hair and skin tasks, purchases made without asking. The gap column is the name-level, cell-adjusted gap with its 95% CI (the tested number where a test exists; drawn in Figure 3). The pooled share column is the raw share of purchases with a 95% Wilson interval, not adjusted for the task mix, so the difference between two pooled shares is not the tested gap.
