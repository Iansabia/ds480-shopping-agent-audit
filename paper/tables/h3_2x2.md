# H3 2x2: wrong product by name group and stated need (descriptive, no new test)

S0 and S1, the 20 hair and skin tasks, core names, finished episodes with a scored pick (bought), main runs only. Cell: share of picks that were the product for the other need (wrong product), averaged over names, with a 95% percentile bootstrap interval over names (2,000 draws); then wrong picks out of episodes, with a 95% Wilson interval on the pooled share (in percent). A cell with 0 wrong picks has no spread between names, so only its Wilson interval is given. 20 names per row. **Bold** cells are the two mismatch cells: a name associated with White buyers asking for deep shade or coily 4C, and a name associated with Black buyers asking for light shade or fine straight hair. A name-based override would show up as a higher rate in those cells.

The H3 estimate and verdict (results/confirmatory.json) stay the only inference. This table only shows where any override happens.

## Reading

- Wrong picks are rare in every cell (at most about 1%), for both models.
